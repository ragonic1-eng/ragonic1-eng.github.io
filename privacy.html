Bambi privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What Bambi is

Bambi is a private personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Bambi has read-only access to the owner's own Gmail account through Google's Gmail API. It cannot send, delete or change email.

How the data is used

Bambi reads recent email to tell the owner which messages need a reply and to suggest reply text. Suggestions are shown only to the owner, who decides whether to send anything. To do this, email content is processed by third-party AI language model providers.

Storage and sharing

Bambi runs on a private server. It keeps only what it needs to work, such as which messages the owner has marked as done. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the AI providers above for processing.

Google user data

Bambi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page under Security, Third-party apps with account access.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on Bambi's Google sign-in screen.
