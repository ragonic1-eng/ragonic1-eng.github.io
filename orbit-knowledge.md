## How Orbit should advise (tour-agent rules)

- Plan around the 2-year-old: one big activity in the morning, a nap or quiet drive after lunch, an easy evening.
- Prefer places with toilets, shade or indoor space, and flat paths for a stroller. Say so when a place is not stroller-friendly.
- Allow buffers: drive times exclude traffic, parking and getting children in and out of the car.
- Prefer fewer, better stops over a packed day. Never suggest cutting a drive time.
- December in the mountains (Cingjing, about 1,700–2,000 m) is cold, especially at night — remind about warm layers.

## Places and lessons learned

- 紅色琉璃瓦草莓園 & SUHU Café (Dahu, Miaoli): raised-bed strawberry picking plus a café. Seasonal; Google Maps showed
  "Opens 14 Nov" for the 2026 season. Farms close on random days in season, so check their Facebook (facebook.com/dahuRED)
  the day before. (checked 2026-10-27, Google Maps + web)
- 童萌農場 (Dahu): hands-on animal farm about 1 km from the strawberry farm, 4.8★ on Google; good for both ages.
  (checked 2026-10-27, Google Maps via Orbit)
- Hobbit Valley (Dahu): 3.7★ on Google and hilly paths — skip with a toddler. (checked 2026-10-27, Google Maps via Orbit)

## Regions on this trip

- Dahu (Miaoli): strawberry capital; season roughly November to April.
- Puli and Cingjing (Nantou): mountain roads; Highway 14A up to Cingjing is winding — motion-sickness bags for the kids.
- Yilan (Jiaoxi, Luodong, Sanxing, Dongshan, Su'ao): farms and night markets; reached from Taipei through the Hsuehshan Tunnel.
- Taipei: final night; Shilin Night Market.
